# Changelog - 2.0.1

## Shadow battle options

Two new settings are available under **Gameplay → Hyper Mode, Reverse Mode & Call**. Both are off by default and can be enabled independently.

- **Allow Hyper Mode Disobedience on Shadow Moves** lets a Pokémon already in Hyper Mode disobey a Shadow move command. Modern rules use the usual 90% disobedience roll. Under Colosseum rules, Shadow Rush can now trigger the same Nature-based outcomes as other moves.
- **Allow Hyper Mode and Reverse Mode Together** lets a Shadow Pokémon enter either mode while the other is active, provided both modes are enabled. It can disobey commands and take Reverse Mode damage on the same turn. The Aura Reader shows both modes on your Pokémon, and one Call clears both.

## Party display and battle fixes

- Shadow Pokémon now show a purple Heart Gauge in the party overlay’s EXP slot. It drains as the heart opens. The EXP bar returns after purification.
- In Radical Cobblemon Trainers battles, if you snag a Pokémon before its trainer’s healing item takes effect, the trainer gets the item back and can send in a replacement. If the snag fails, the heal still goes through.
- Ally moves aimed at a Pokémon that was just snagged are canceled. Revival Blessing also skips snagged Pokémon.
- NPC trainers now respect the Shadow Mega Evolution setting when choosing a move, keeping the Pokémon’s form consistent in battle.
- The wild Shadow spawn sound now plays after one second if the Pokémon is still present, alive, wild, and Shadow. A spawn that disappears immediately no longer triggers the sound.

## Interface fixes

- Text now stays readable with Menu Background Blur enabled in the Aura Reader and Purification PC tutorials, as well as in Purification PC and Umbra Infuser tooltips.
